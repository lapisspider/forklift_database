About SSC Forklift Database

Comprehensive equipment specifications, AI-powered lookups, and kit compatibility tracking for material handling operations.

Safety Systems & Controls (SSC)

SSC specializes in designing and manufacturing advanced safety systems for mobile equipment, with a focus on forklifts and material handling vehicles. Since 1994, we've been partnering with OEMs, dealers, and industrial operators to deliver safety solutions that strengthen productivity and protect people.

Our Products

  • PACE-ONE™ Speed Limiter & G2 Speed Control — Zone-based speed management with ECU integration
  • DOT-LOK™ Shift Inhibitor — Automatic transmission control for safe operations
  • Ignition Interlock — Seat occupancy verification and operator presence detection
  • PED-LOK™ Pedal Lock — Prevents unintended acceleration
  • Trans-Stop™ Transmission Interlock — Electromechanical transmission safety
  • SPEEDZONE & RF Transmitters — Geofenced zone-based speed control
  • μCal™ Calibration Tool — ECU mapping and vehicle parameter tuning

Forklift Database

This database provides complete specifications for forklifts across major manufacturers including:

  • Toyota · Crown · Nissan/Unicarrier
  • Hyster · Yale · Linde
  • Bobcat/Doosan · CAT/Mitsubishi
  • Clark · Hyundai · Komatsu
  • BYD · Heli · Jungheinrich
  • And more...

Each model includes capacity, fuel type, OSHA class, production years, and compatibility tracking for SSC safety kits.
