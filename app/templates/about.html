Safety Systems & Controls (SSC)

SSC designs safety products for mobile equipment — systems that help prevent accidents, curb equipment misuse, and limit injury and damage in demanding industrial settings. The company sells primarily through OEMs and equipment dealers, while also supporting the operators who use the systems day to day.

Our Products

  • PACE-ONE™ G2 Speed Limiter — Forklift speed limiting with ECU integration
  • PACE-ONE™ ZSC Zone Speed Control — Speed limits that change by zone
  • RF Transmitters Nomad™ — Transmitters that define the speed-control zones
  • DOT-LOK™ G2 Shift Inhibitor — Blocks shifting outside safe conditions
  • Ignition Interlock — Seatbelt interlock; the truck won't start unbuckled
  • Speedzone G2 — Mechanical governor conversion
  • Critical Systems Control™ — Transmission temperature monitoring
  • μCal™ — Calibration and software updates for SSC controllers
  • PED-LOK™ — Bus loading brake control
  • Trans-Stop™ Transmission Interlock — Electromechanical transmission safety
  • Mast Height Speed Control — Overhead mast warning and speed reduction
  • Active Speed Control — Adaptive speed control

Forklift Database

  • Toyota · Crown · Nissan/Unicarrier
  • Hyster · Yale · Linde
