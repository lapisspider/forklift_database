About the SSC Forklift Database

Equipment specifications, serial decoding, and kit compatibility for the trucks Safety Systems & Controls builds for.

Safety Systems & Controls

SSC designs safety products for mobile equipment — systems that help prevent accidents, curb equipment misuse, and limit injury and damage in demanding industrial settings. The company sells primarily through OEMs and equipment dealers, while also supporting the operators who use the systems day to day.

How it is used

Lookup by model or serial
Checks the catalog first; if nothing matches, it searches the web and proposes a record for review. Serial numbers decode offline, free.
Kit compatibility
Each kit-to-forklift connection carries a validation tier, so you can tell a signed-off installation from an editor's first pass.
Full equipment coverage
Counterbalance trucks, reach trucks, warehouse gear, tow tractors and heavy lift.
OSHA classification
Class I to VII, assigned from the truck's power, tyre and configuration.
